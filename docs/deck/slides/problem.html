The problem

Traffic, accidents and enforcement run in separate systems

Each team sees a piece of the picture. Nobody sees the chain that connects a violation to a crash to a jam to a fine.

01

Congestion

Monitored in one tool. Operators see that traffic is slow, not why it is slow.

02

Accidents

Handled in a separate dispatch workflow, with no link to the traffic impact they cause.

03

Enforcement

Fines are issued from a back office, with little context and a heavy review load.
