    About this demonstrator

    IntelliTwin is a static, browser-only demonstrator of real-time transportation intelligence and predictive simulation. Every traffic value shown is synthetic: it comes from an in-browser volume-delay model with assumed demand profiles, not from sensors, counts or any live feed.

    Corridor alignments are schematic. This is not an official model of any UAE authority and must not be used for planning, operational or safety decisions.
